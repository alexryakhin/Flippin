Type It Once, Hear It Right

Type a word or phrase in your language and the translation fills itself in. Tap the speaker on either side of a card to hear it spoken.

  • Automatic translation as you type, on-device with Apple Translation when the language is installed
  • One tap on swap to type in either language
  • Notes, tags and favorites keep what you learn organized
  • Free Google and system voices with adjustable speed; Premium adds nearly 1,000 natural Speechify voices

Vocab Games You'll Love

Short sessions that feel like a game: a progress bar, combos, instant feedback and a summary with your accuracy at the end.

  • Flashcards: recall, show the answer, then Again or Got it Free
  • Match: six pairs against the clock Free
  • Multiple choice, fill in the blank, listen and type Premium
  • Cards you miss come back a few cards later, so they stick

Speaking Practice, Out Loud

Hold the mic, say the phrase, and Flippin checks it with speech recognition. Saying it out loud is how it becomes yours.

  • Say phrases out loud and get them checked, on-device when available
  • Can't talk right now? Type the answer instead
  • Hear any phrase again first, as many times as you like

Premium Speak, Listen and Type are Premium practice modes.

Keep Your Streak Going

A daily streak, a weekly goal and friendly reminders turn a few minutes a day into real progress.

  • A streak calendar with your best streak and the next milestone
  • A weekly goal of 10, 25, 50 or 100 cards, with a celebration when you reach it
  • “Review due cards” starts with what's due, then your mistakes, then new cards
  • Daily study and difficult-card reminders at the times you choose

AI Phrasebooks in Seconds

Describe a topic, like ordering at a restaurant, and get a ready-to-study set of 10 to 20 cards for your language pair and level.

  • Edit any card, pick the ones you want, or regenerate
  • Every set is saved as a draft, so nothing gets lost
  • An AI learning coach with a daily insight based on your own progress

Premium AI collections and the AI coach are part of Premium; free users can try an example set.

Questions? Answers

Do I need an account?
No. Flippin works without an account and your cards sync through iCloud. Sign in with Apple is optional: it syncs your profile, streak and subscription across devices. The AI features need it so your Premium status can be checked.
What's free?
Up to 40 cards in one language pair, automatic translation, Google and system voices, 11 phrase collections, Flashcards and Match practice, your streak, weekly goal and overview stats, and iCloud sync. Premium unlocks everything else.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. With iCloud on, your cards and practice history sync between them.
Which languages can I learn?
Any pair of 32 languages, and the app itself is available in 34 languages. See the full list.
How do I cancel Premium?
In your App Store account settings: iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Flippin. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.
