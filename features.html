Features

Everything you need to build a daily language habit—without making the app feel complicated.

Smart flashcards (fast creation)

Create flashcards quickly so your time goes into studying—not formatting. Add tags and notes to make cards memorable, plus images and emoji with Premium.

Learn more: Smart flashcards

Starter collections

Never start from an empty deck. Pick your interests during setup and Flippin adds a few starter collections for you. 11 preset collections (one per category) are free, and Premium unlocks all 30.

Auto‑translation (32 languages)

Translate words and phrases as you create cards—ideal when you’re collecting vocabulary from reading, shows, or travel phrases.

Learn more: Supported languages

Pronunciation with text‑to‑speech

Hear the correct pronunciation and repeat out loud. Free Google and system voices are included; Premium adds nearly 1,000 natural Speechify voices.

Learn more: Pronunciation

Progress analytics (stay consistent)

Track streaks and basic stats for free. Premium adds charts, mastery breakdowns, detailed analytics and a daily AI learning coach.

Learn more: Analytics

iCloud sync

With iCloud on, your flashcards and practice history stay in sync across your Apple devices—no account needed. Optional Sign in with Apple also syncs your profile, streak and subscription.

Premium features

The free tier gives you up to 40 cards in one language pair. Premium is for learners who want more:

  • Unlimited cards and all 30 preset collections
  • AI collection generator and AI learning coach
  • Card images and emoji mode
  • Switch between language pairs
  • Multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank quiz sessions
  • Detailed analytics
  • Natural Speechify voices (nearly 1,000)
  • All animated backgrounds and a custom accent color

Premium costs $4.99/month, $29.99/year (with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan) or $79.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in USD and vary by country or region.
