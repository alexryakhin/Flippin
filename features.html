Features

Everything You Need to Speak

Flashcards you can hear, games that make review fun, and practice that gets you saying the words out loud. Free to start, with Premium when you want more.

Start in Seconds

Pick your languages, level, goal and interests, and Flippin builds your first deck for you.

  • A starter pack of ready-made phrase collections based on your interests, so your deck is never empty
  • Try three real cards right in the setup: flip, listen, and mark them Again or Got it
  • No account needed; Sign in with Apple is optional

Make Cards Fast

Type a word or phrase and the translation fills itself in after a moment. Edit it if you like; your changes are kept.

  • Apple's on-device translation when the language is installed, otherwise Google
  • Swap to type in either language with one tap
  • Notes, up to five tags and favorites on every card
  • Card images from Pexels and emoji, typed or suggested by AI Premium

Hear Every Word

Tap the speaker on either side of a card, in any list, or during practice.

  • Free Google voices (nine English accents) and the system voices, which also work offline
  • Adjustable speed from 0.5× to 2×
  • Nearly 1,000 natural Speechify voices with filters, previews and recommendations per language Premium

Pronunciation tips

Flip, Swipe, Study

Your deck as a stack of 3D paper cards. Tap to flip, swipe for the next one, shuffle to study again.

  • Every card shows if it's New, Learning or Mastered
  • Filter by favorites, difficult cards or a tag
  • Learning mode shows the new language first; Traveler mode shows yours first
  • Emoji Mode puts emoji on the back instead of a translation Premium

How to make great flashcards

Seven Ways to Practice

Practice picks the cards that need it: due cards first, then mistakes, then new ones. Or practice your mistakes, or your weak, learning or strong cards.

ModeWhat you doPlan
FlashcardsRecall the meaning, show the answer, then Again or Got it (or swipe)Free
MatchMatch six pairs in two columns against the clockFree
Multiple choicePick the right translation from four tilesPremium
Fill in the blankComplete the sentence, with its meaning shown underneathPremium
ListenHear the phrase (replay it, or slow it down) and choose the meaningPremium
TypeType the phrase; small slips in accents or punctuation count as almost rightPremium
SpeakHold to record and say it; speech recognition checks it, with typing as a fallbackPremium

Every session ends with a summary: correct answers, accuracy, time, what's due soon and your weekly goal. Cards you miss come back a few cards later, and each answer updates the card's mastery and schedules its next review.

Streaks & Goals

Small daily wins, visible at a glance.

  • Study streak with a month calendar, your best streak and the next milestone (7, 30, 100, 365 days)
  • Weekly goal of 10, 25, 50 or 100 cards, with a ring for every day of the week
  • Daily study reminder and a difficult-card reminder, at the times you choose

See Your Progress

Free: your streak, weekly goal, total study time, cards studied and sessions today, and lifetime accuracy.

  • Study-time charts by week, month or year Premium
  • Mastery breakdown, accuracy trends, milestones and badges Premium
  • An AI learning coach with a daily insight and recommendations based on your week Premium

How to use your progress analytics

AI Phrasebooks

Describe what you want to learn, choose 10, 15 or 20 cards, and get a ready-to-study collection for your language pair and level.

  • Tap any card to edit it, pick which ones to add, regenerate or edit the prompt
  • Your last 20 results are kept as drafts, also offline
  • Free users can browse an example set before upgrading

Premium AI features need Premium and Sign in with Apple. AI-generated content can contain mistakes.

And the Details

iCloud sync

Cards, tags and practice history sync across your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud, with or without an account.

Optional account

Sign in with Apple syncs your profile, streak and subscription across devices. Signing out keeps your cards.

Light and dark

A bright sky by day and a starry night in dark mode, following your system or your choice.

34 app languages

The app speaks your language too, with right-to-left layouts for Arabic and Hebrew.

Free and Premium

The free plan includes up to 40 cards in one language pair. Premium removes the limits and adds everything marked Premium above.

Premium costs $4.99/month, $29.99/year (with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers on the yearly plan) or $79.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in USD and vary by country or region; the App Store shows your price before you buy. See the Terms of Use.
