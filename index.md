# Flippin — Speak a new language every day

Flippin turns the phrases you'll actually say into smart flashcards you can hear, flip and practice in a few minutes a day. For iPhone and iPad (version 2.0, requires iOS 18 or later). Learn any pair of 32 languages.

## Highlights

- **Type it once, hear it right:** automatic translation as you type; tap the speaker on either side of a card to hear it.
- **Vocab games:** Flashcards and Match (free); Multiple choice, Fill in the blank, Listen and Type (Premium).
- **Speaking practice:** say phrases out loud and get them checked with speech recognition (Premium).
- **Streaks and goals:** daily streak, weekly goal of 10/25/50/100 cards, study and difficult-card reminders.
- **AI phrasebooks (Premium):** describe a topic and get a ready-to-study set of 10 to 20 cards; AI learning coach.
- **32 languages, any pair:** 30 ready-made phrase collections in every language, 11 of them free.

## Pricing

**Free** — no account needed: up to 40 cards in one language pair, automatic translation, Google and system voices, 11 preset collections (one per topic), Flashcards and Match practice, streak, weekly goal and overview stats, iCloud sync.

**Premium** — **$4.99/month**, **$29.99/year** (7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, yearly plan only) or **$79.99 lifetime** (one-time purchase). Prices are in USD and vary by country or region; limited-time offers such as $19.99/year (no trial) may appear in the app. Includes unlimited cards, all 30 preset collections, the AI collection generator and AI learning coach, Speak/Listen/Type/Multiple choice/Fill in the blank practice, nearly 1,000 natural Speechify voices, card images, AI emoji suggestions and Emoji Mode, switching language pairs, and detailed analytics. See [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.html). Cancel anytime in App Store settings.

## FAQ (short)

- **Account:** Not required. Optional Sign in with Apple syncs your profile, streak and subscription across devices (AI features require it).
- **Devices:** iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later; iCloud syncs your cards across them, with or without an account.
- **Languages:** 32 learnable languages, any pair; the app is available in 34 languages — see [Languages](languages.html).
- **Support:** [support@flippin.app](mailto:support@flippin.app) or [Support](support.html).
