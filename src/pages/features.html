Seven Ways to Practice

Practice picks the cards that need it: due cards first, then mistakes, then new ones. Or practice your mistakes, or your weak, learning or strong cards.

{{@phone practice|Practice tab with Review due cards, mistakes and practice modes|tilt-l}}{{@ill trophy|ill-tr bob}}
ModeWhat you doPlan
FlashcardsRecall the meaning, show the answer, then Again or Got it (or swipe)Free
MatchMatch six pairs in two columns against the clockFree
Multiple choicePick the right translation from four tilesPremium
Fill in the blankComplete the sentence, with its meaning shown underneathPremium
ListenHear the phrase (replay it, or slow it down) and choose the meaningPremium
TypeType the phrase; small slips in accents or punctuation count as almost rightPremium
SpeakHold to record and say it; speech recognition checks it, with typing as a fallbackPremium

Every session ends with a summary: correct answers, accuracy, time, what's due soon and your weekly goal. Cards you miss come back a few cards later, and each answer updates the card's mastery and schedules its next review.

Free and Premium

The free plan includes up to 40 cards in one language pair. Premium removes the limits and adds everything marked Premium above.

Premium costs $4.99/month, $29.99/year (with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers on the yearly plan) or $79.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in USD and vary by country or region; the App Store shows your price before you buy. See the Terms of Use.
