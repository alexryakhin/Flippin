A 20-second loop

  1. Play the phrase.
  2. Say it out loud, once or twice.
  3. Move on. Come back to it tomorrow.

Short and frequent beats long and rare, especially on busy days.

When audio helps most

  • New words whose spelling doesn't tell you how they sound
  • Words that sound alike but mean different things
  • Whole phrases you want to say naturally, with the right rhythm
