What to look at

  • Streak: a light daily commitment. One short session keeps it going.
  • Weekly goal: how many cards you want to review this week (10, 25, 50 or 100).
  • Accuracy and difficult cards: what needs another look, or a better note.

Don't overthink it

Use your stats to decide what the next few minutes should be. Spend more time reviewing cards than looking at charts.
