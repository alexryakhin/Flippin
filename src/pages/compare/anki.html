Choose Flippin if…

  • You want to start in minutes, with ready-made phrase collections in 32 languages
  • You like automatic translation and pronunciation built into card creation
  • You enjoy game-like practice: Match, multiple choice, listening, typing and speaking exercises
  • You want streaks, a weekly goal and simple progress stats to stay consistent
  • You use an iPhone or iPad

Choose Anki if…

  • You want maximum control and customization: note types, templates, add-ons and scheduling settings
  • You're happy to spend time building and tuning your own decks
  • You study across many platforms and subjects, not only languages

In short

Anki is a power tool. Flippin is built to be a great default for language learners who want to start quickly, hear every phrase and keep going every day.
