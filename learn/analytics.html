Progress You Can See

The goal is consistency: keep your streak, hit your weekly goal, and review the right cards at the right time.

What to look at

  • Streak: a light daily commitment. One short session keeps it going.
  • Weekly goal: how many cards you want to review this week (10, 25, 50 or 100).
  • Accuracy and difficult cards: what needs another look, or a better note.

Don't overthink it

Use your stats to decide what the next few minutes should be. Spend more time reviewing cards than looking at charts.

What Flippin shows

Free your weekly goal with a ring for every day, your streak with a month calendar, best streak and next milestone, total study time, cards studied and sessions today, and lifetime accuracy.

Premium study-time charts (week, month, year), a mastery breakdown, detailed analytics with accuracy trends, milestones and badges, and an AI learning coach with a daily insight and recommendations based on your own week.
