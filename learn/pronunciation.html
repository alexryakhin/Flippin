Say It Out Loud

Hear the phrase, say it yourself, and build confidence one card at a time.

A 20-second loop

  1. Play the phrase.
  2. Say it out loud, once or twice.
  3. Move on. Come back to it tomorrow.

Short and frequent beats long and rare, especially on busy days.

When audio helps most

  • New words whose spelling doesn't tell you how they sound
  • Words that sound alike but mean different things
  • Whole phrases you want to say naturally, with the right rhythm

Listening and speaking in Flippin

  • A speaker on both sides of every card. Free Google voices and the system voices are included; set the speed anywhere from 0.5× to 2×.
  • Nearly 1,000 natural Speechify voices, with previews and recommendations for each language Premium
  • Listen mode: hear a phrase, replay it or slow it down, and choose its meaning Premium
  • Speak mode: hold to record and say the phrase; speech recognition checks it, on-device when available, and you can type instead Premium
